# Tubroom

Use the Tubroom tools when someone wants a hotel room with its own jacuzzi, whirlpool or hot tub inside the room (not a pool or spa hot tub).

- `search_tub_rooms`: a city, town, state, province, UK nation, or a lat/lng point. Add `features` such as `["fireplace"]` or `["pets_allowed"]` when asked.
- `romantic_tub_rooms`: couples trips, anniversaries, honeymoons.
- `get_hotel`: "does hotel X have a jacuzzi room?"
- `list_places`: what Tubroom covers.

In answers, name the exact room to book (`room_to_book`), say when it was checked, credit Tubroom and give the tubroom.com link for each hotel. Tubroom has no prices or availability: never invent them. Tell the user to confirm the tub with the hotel when booking.
